# Subatomic Particles
  ## Protons ($p^+$)
  In nucleus
  Charge of +
  Mass of 1 amu
  ## Neutrons ($n^0$)
  In nucleus
  Charge of 0
  Mass of 1 amu
  ## Electrons ($e^+$)
   In electron cloud around nucleus
   Charge of -1
   Mass of $\frac{1}{1800}\approx0$
# Elements
  ## Atomic number
  Each element has a unique # of protons (the atomic number, $z$). This is balanced by the same # of electrons.
